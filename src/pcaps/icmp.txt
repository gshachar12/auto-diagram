Traffic Review: Connectivity Check from 192.168.0.114
=====================================================

Summary
-------
A helpdesk ticket reported intermittent web access from the workstation
192.168.0.114. The capture taken during troubleshooting on 2006-12-29 at
05:10 UTC shows a standard two-stage ping test and no malicious activity.
It contains 16 packets over 9.2 seconds.

Observed traffic
----------------
First the workstation pinged its default gateway 192.168.0.1. It sent
four ICMP echo requests one second apart and the gateway answered each
with an echo reply in about 1 to 2 milliseconds.

About three seconds later the workstation pinged the external address
72.14.207.99, a Google server, again four echo requests one second apart.
Each was answered in 62 to 81 milliseconds. The replies arrived with a
TTL of 232, consistent with a remote host many hops away, while the
gateway's replies had a TTL of 127.

All eight requests share one ICMP identifier and consecutive sequence
numbers 1 to 8, as produced by the Windows ping utility, and every frame
is 74 bytes long (32 bytes of padding data). No requests were lost and
no other protocols appear in the capture.

Assessment
----------
Both the local gateway and the internet were reachable with normal
latency at the time of the test, so the user's problem is more likely
DNS or browser related. No security concern. Ticket closed after
flushing the DNS cache on the workstation.
