A single source host sends TCP connection-initiation packets to a single
target host across a range of different destination ports, in rapid,
evenly-paced succession roughly a tenth of a second apart. Each probe
consists only of a TCP SYN packet, and no completed handshake -- no
SYN-ACK response, no final ACK -- appears anywhere in this capture. The
specific ports probed are concentrated among widely-recognized standard
service ports, including SMB, NetBIOS, MSRPC, SSH, Telnet, and FTP,
rather than a purely sequential sweep of the full port range.
